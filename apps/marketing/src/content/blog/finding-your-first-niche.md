Finding a niche isn't about brainstorming in a vacuum. It's about reading signals
that already exist — search demand, community momentum, and the gap between what
people ask for and what's actually being built.

## Start with momentum, not ideas

The best niches show up as **rising** interest, not peak interest. By the time a
topic is saturated, the opportunity has moved on. Look for:

- Communities growing faster than their tooling
- Questions asked repeatedly with no good answer
- Adjacent markets bleeding demand into an unserved segment

## Validate before you build

A niche worth pursuing survives three questions:

1. Can you reach the audience without paying for every click?
2. Is the pain frequent enough that people actively search for relief?
3. Is there room — is the space *un-saturated* enough to enter?
